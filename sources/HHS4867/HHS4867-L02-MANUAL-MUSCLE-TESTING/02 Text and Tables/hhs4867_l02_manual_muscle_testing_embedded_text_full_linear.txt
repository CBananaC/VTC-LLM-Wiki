Functional Movement Science (HAS4867)
Manual Muscle Testing (MMT)
Manual muscle testing is a procedure for the evaluation of the strength of
individual muscles and muscle groups based on the performance of a movement
in relation to manual resistance.
Patients are asked to hold a position against a therapist’s resistance.
Resistance needs to be applied gradually in the opposite direction of muscle
being tested.
Both sides should be tested to provide a comparison.
Muscle Strength Grading Scale (Oxford Scale)
• Grade 0: No contraction
• Grade1: Visible/palpable muscle contraction but no movement
• Grade2: Completes ROM with gravity eliminated
• Grade 3: Completes ROM against gravity only
• Grade 4: Completes ROM against gravity with some resistance
• Grade 5: Completes ROM against gravity with full resistance
Testing position
• Anti-Gravity – for muscle strength Grade 3 or above
• Gravity Eliminated – for muscle strength Grade 2 or below
Shoulder Joint
1. Shoulder flexion
Subject Test Position
Sitting with arm by the side
Action
Ask the subject to raise arm up to 90°, and hold the position against therapist’s
manual resistance
Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner stabilizes the subject’s scapula and gives the resistance above the
elbow
The MMT grading is:
Grade 3 - if the subject can hold the test position only
Grade 4 - if the subject can hold the position against moderate resistance
Grade 5 – if the subject can hold the position against full resistance
Gravity Eliminated Position
Side lying with the testing arm supported by the examiner, and check the2
subject whether he/she can do a full ROM or not (Grade 2)
2. Shoulder Extension
Subject Test Position
Prone lying with arm by the side, palm facing up
Action
Ask the subject to extend arm to end range, and hold the position against
therapist’s manual resistance
Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner stabilizes the subject’s scapula and gives the resistance above the
elbow
Gravity Eliminated Position
Side lying with the testing arm supported by the examiner, and check the
subject whether he/she can do a full ROM or not (Grade 2)
3. Shoulder Abduction
Subject Test Position
Sitting with arm by the side
Action

Supine lying with the testing arm supported by the bed, and check the subject

4. Shoulder Internal Rotation
Subject Test Position
Prone lying with arm abducting to 90°, elbow flexed to 90°, with palm facing

Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner stabilizes the subject’s humerus and gives the resistance above the
wrist

Wrist Joint
1. Wrist Flexion
Subject Test Position
Sitting with forearm supinated, resting on the table
Action
Ask the subject to flex the wrist, and hold the position against therapist’s
manual resistance
Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner stabilizes the subject’s forearm and gives the resistance on the
palmar surface of hand
Gravity Eliminated Position
Ask the subject to flex the wrist, with the forearm supported & hand resting on
the medial border, check whether he/she can slide the hand along the table or
not (Grade 2)
2. Wrist Extension
Subject Test Position
Sitting with forearm pronated, resting on the table
Action
Ask the subject to extend the wrist, and hold the position against therapist’s
manual resistance
Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner stabilizes the subject’s forearm and gives the resistance on the dorsal
surface of the hand
Gravity Eliminated Position
Ask the subject to extend the wrist, with the forearm supported & hand resting
on the medial border, check whether he/she can slide the hand along the table
or not (Grade 2)

Hip Joint
1. Hip Flexion
Subject Test Position
Sitting on high plinth, legs over the edge of the plinth
Action
Ask the subject to flex the hip, and hold the position against therapist’s manual
resistance
Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner gives the resistance proximal to the knee
Gravity Eliminated Position
Side lying with the subject’s leg supported by the examiner, and the knee is
allowed to flex preventing hamstrings tension.
Check whether he/she can flex the hip through the range of motion or not
(Grade 2)
2. Hip Extension
Subject Test Position
Prone lying with legs extended
Action
Ask the subject to extend the hip through range of motion, and hold the
position against therapist’s manual resistance
Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner stabilizes the subject’s pelvis and gives the resistance proximal to the
knee joint
Gravity Eliminated Position
Side lying with the subject’s leg supported by the examiner, with the testing hip
flexed, knee extended.
Check the subject whether he/she can do a full ROM or not (Grade 2)

knee joint
Gravity Eliminated Position
Supine lying with the testing leg extended, and check whether he/she can do a
full ROM or not (Grade 2)6

Prone lying with legs extended, feet over the edge of plinth
Action

Examiner’s Resistance
Examiner gives the resistance against the forefoot as well as the calcaneus
Gravity Eliminated Position
Ask the subject to plantarflex the ankle in side lying position, with the testing
leg on the plinth, and see whether he/she can move the foot along the plinth or
not (Grade 2)8
