Modified Functional Ambulation Categories
CategoriesStage Definition
I Lyer
Unable to walk / unable to sit without support>1min /
assist to sit
II Sitter
Sit without support >1min / unable to walk with 1 person
assistance
III
Dependent
walker
Walk with 1 person assistance – level ground (continuous
manual contact to support body weight)
IV
Assisted
walker
Walk with 1 person assistance – level ground (continuous
or intermittent manual contact)
V
Supervised
walker
Walk with standby guarding from 1 person – level ground
VI Indoor walker
Transfer, turn & walk on level ground independently,
needs assistance on stairs/inclines
VII
Outdoor
walker
Walk independently on level or non-level surfaces
This classification does not take account of any aid used.
