                   Gait cycle 步態週期
The gait cycle is defined as the time interval between two successive occurrence of
one of the repetitive events of walking.

步態循環定義為兩次連續發生行走重複事件的時間間隔
Stance phase & Swing phase
站立階段和擺動階段

•   Swing phase 擺動階段
    • when the foot is moving forward through the air
       當腳在空中向前移動時

•   Stance phase 站立階段
    • Support phase, last from initial contact to toe off
       支撐階段，從初始接觸到腳趾離地的過程
                 Gait cycle 步態週期
