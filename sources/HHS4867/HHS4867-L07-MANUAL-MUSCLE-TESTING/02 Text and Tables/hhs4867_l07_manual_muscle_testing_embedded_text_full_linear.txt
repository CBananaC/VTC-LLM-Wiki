手動肌肉測試
• Assessment of muscle strength
• Manual muscle testing is a
procedure for the evaluation of the
function and strength of individual
muscles and muscle groups based on
the effective performanceof a
movement in relation to the forces of
gravityand manual resistance.
• Dynamometry is a method of
strength testing using
sophisticated strength measuring
devices (e.g.,hand-grip, hand-
held, fixed, and isokinetic
dynamometry).
• 肌肉力量評估

肌肉及肌肉群功能和力量的程
序，根據有效的運動表現來評
估其對抗重力和手動抵抗的能
力。

如何執行MMT
• Patients are asked to hold a
position against a therapist’s
resistance.
• It is important to stabilize proximal
part of the area being tested to
reduce compensatory action by
muscles other than those being
tested
• Resistance needs to be applied
gradually ("meet my resistance" or
"don't let me move you") in the
opposite direction of muscle being
tested
• Both sides should be tested to
provide a comparison
• 患者被要求在治療師的抵
抗下保持一個姿勢
• 穩定被測試區域的近端部
分很重要，以減少其他肌
肉的補償動作
• 抵抗需要逐漸施加（「抵
抗我的力量」或「不讓我
移動你」），方向與被測
試的肌肉相反
• 應該檢測雙側以提供比較。T estingPosition測試位置
