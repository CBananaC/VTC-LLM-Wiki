Muscle Strength Grading Scale (Oxford Scale)
肌肉力量分級量表（牛津量表）
 0     No contraction
       無收縮
 1     Visible/palpable muscle contraction but no movement
       可見/可觸及的肌肉收縮，但無活動
 2     Movement with gravity eliminated
       消除重力後的活動
 3     Movement against gravity only
       僅對抗重力的活動
 4     Movement against gravity with some resistance
       對抗重力且有部分抵抗的活動
 5     Movement against gravity with full resistance
       對抗重力並具有完全抵抗的活動
5    subject completes ROM against gravity with maximal resistance
     受試者以最大阻力完成對抗重力的 ROM
4+   completes ROM against gravity with moderate-maximal resistance
     以中等-最大阻力完成對抗重力的 ROM
4    completes ROM against gravity with moderate resistance
     以中等阻力完成對抗重力的 ROM
4-   completes ROM against gravity with minimal-moderate resistance
     以最小-中等阻力完成 ROM 抵抗重力
3+   completes ROM against gravity with only minimal resistance
     以最小的阻力完成對抗重力的 ROM
3    completes ROM against gravity without manual resistance
     在沒有手動阻力的情況下完成對抗重力的 ROM
3-   does not complete the range of motion against gravity, but does complete more than half of the range
     沒有完成抵抗重力的運動範圍，但確實完成了一半以上的範圍
2+   is able to initiate movement against gravity
     能夠啟動對抗重力的運動
2    completes range of motion with gravity eliminated
     消除重力，完成運動範圍
2-   does not complete ROM in a gravity eliminated position
     未在重力消除位置完成 ROM
1    muscle contraction can be palpated, but there is no joint movement
     可以觸診肌肉收縮，但沒有關節運動
0    patient demonstrates no palpable muscle contraction
     患者沒有明顯的肌肉收縮

 • Patients are asked to hold a            • 患者被要求在治療師的抵
   position against a therapist’s            抗下保持一個姿勢
   resistance.
 • It is important to stabilize proximal   • 穩定被測試區域的近端部
   part of the area being tested to          分很重要，以減少其他肌
   reduce compensatory action by             肉的補償動作
   muscles other than those being
   tested
 • Resistance needs to be applied          • 抵抗需要逐漸施加（「抵
   gradually ("meet my resistance" or        抗我的力量」或「不讓我
   "don't let me move you") in the           移動你」），方向與被測
   opposite direction of muscle being        試的肌肉相反
   tested
 • Both sides should be tested to          • 應該檢測雙側以提供比較。
   provide a comparison
