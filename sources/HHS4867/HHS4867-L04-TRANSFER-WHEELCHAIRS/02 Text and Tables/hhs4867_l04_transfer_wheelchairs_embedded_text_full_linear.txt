使用輪椅的安全提示
➢ Always LOCK the brakes when
you leave the patient or when not
being pushed.
➢ Avoid leaning forwards or
bending to reach low objects
➢ Never stand on the footplates
➢ Use safety straps if provided
(especially when going outdoors
or for patient with poor sitting
balance)
➢ Make sure:
➢ Feet safely on the footplates
➢ Elbows, all clothing, rugs or
bags are tucked in safely.
➢ Always let the client know
what you are going to do
➢ 當您離開病人或不被推動時，
始終鎖住剎車。
➢ 避免身體前傾或彎腰以接觸低
矮的物體
➢ 永遠不要站在腳踏板上
➢ 使用安全帶（尤其是在戶外或
坐姿平衡不佳的患者時）
➢ 確保：
➢ 雙腳安全地放在腳踏板上
➢ 肘部、所有衣服、地毯或包

Getting into the wheelchair
• LOCK the brakes!
• Fold up the footplates and swing sway.
• Stand with back of leg against the seat
and press down on armrest.
• Lower and place foot on footplates.
• Pay attention to keep the heel clear of
the front casters.
Getting out of the wheelchair
• LOCK the brakes!
• Fold up the footplates and swing sway.
• Make sure walking aids(if needed) is
ready.
• Slightly bend trunk forwards, standing
up by pressing down on the armrest
坐上輪椅
• 鎖上剎車！
• 摺疊腳踏板並擺動搖擺。
• 站立，腿後靠靠座椅，按下扶手。
• 降低並將腳放在腳踏板上。
• 注意保持腳跟遠離前腳輪。
從輪椅下來
• 鎖上剎車！
• 摺疊腳踏板並擺動搖擺。
• 確保助行器（如果需要）已準備就
緒。
• 軀幹稍微向前彎曲，按下扶手站起
來Getting down a kerb
