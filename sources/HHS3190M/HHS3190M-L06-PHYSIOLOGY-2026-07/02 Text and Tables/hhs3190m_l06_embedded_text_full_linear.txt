• Pulmonary ventilation - “Breathing” 肺通氣 - “呼吸”
• consists of two phases 由兩個階段組成

• (1) Muscle movements 肌肉運動
• (2) Volume changes 容積變化
• (3) Pressure changes 壓力變化
• (4) Flows of air 空氣流動

• Alveolar ventilation (AV) 肺泡通氣量 :
• Flow of gases into and out of respiratory zone in 1 minute
1分鐘內流入和流出呼吸區的氣體量
• Volume of dead space is taken into account in AV 肺泡通氣量考慮了死腔的容積
• Better indicator of effective ventilation 有效通氣的更好指標
• AV = (TV - Dead space) × Respiratory Rate
肺泡通氣量 = (潮氣容積 - 死腔容積) × 呼吸頻率
• Example: TV = 500 ml; Respiratory Rate = 14 breaths/min; Dead space = 150 ml
例子：潮氣容積 = 500 毫升;呼吸頻率 = 14 次呼吸/分鐘;死腔 = 150 毫升
AV = (500 – 150) × 14 = 4900 ml/min
肺泡通氣量 = (500 – 150) × 14 = 4900 毫升/分鐘
