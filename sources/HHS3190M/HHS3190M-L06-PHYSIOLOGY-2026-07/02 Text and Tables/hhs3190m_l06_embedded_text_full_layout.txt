Anatomy of the Respiratory System
呼吸系統的解剖
• Respiratory zone structure 呼吸區結構
• The alveolar walls are composed of 1 layer of squamous epithelial cells, called “type I
  cells”. 肺泡壁由1層鱗狀上皮細胞組成，稱為「I型細胞」。
• The external surface of type I cells is covered by a thin basement membrane.
  I 型細胞的外表面被一層薄的基底膜覆蓋。
• A dense network of thin-wall capillaries surrounds the alveolus.
  密集的薄壁毛細血管網絡圍繞著肺泡。

• Together, the alveolar wall, basement membrane, and the capillary wall
 form the respiratory membrane.
 肺泡壁、基底膜和毛細血管壁共同形成呼吸膜。
• Gas exchange occurs by simple diffusion across the respiratory membrane.
   氣體交換通過穿過呼吸膜的簡單擴散而發生。

Mechanics of Breathing 呼吸力學
• Pulmonary ventilation - “Breathing” 肺通氣 - “呼吸”
 • consists of two phases 由兩個階段組成
 • Inspiration: gases flow into lungs 吸氣：氣體流入肺部
 • Expiration: gases exit lungs 呼氣：氣體從肺部排出
• Important basic sequence: 重要的基本順序：
 • (1) Muscle movements 肌肉運動
 • (2) Volume changes 容積變化
 • (3) Pressure changes 壓力變化
 • (4) Flows of air 空氣流動

• Anatomical dead space解剖死腔 :
 • Volume of air that does not take part in gas exchange
  不參與氣體交換的空氣量
• Consists of air that remains in conducting zone 由留在傳導區的空氣組成
• Average 150 ml out of 500 ml TV 在潮氣容積為500 ml之情況下，平均值為150 ml
• Alveolar dead space 肺泡死腔 :
 • Should be zero in healthy person 在健康人仕身上應該為零
 • Space occupied by nonfunctional alveoli 非功能性肺泡佔據的空間
 • Can be due to collapse or obstruction 可能由於塌陷或阻塞
• Total dead space 死腔總容量 :
 • sum of anatomical and alveolar dead space 解剖死腔及肺泡死腔之總和

Mechanics of Breathing 呼吸力學
• Minute Ventilation (MV) 每分鐘通氣量 :
 • total amount of gas that flows into or out of respiratory tract in 1 minute
  1 分鐘內流入或流出呼吸道的氣體總量
• Normal at rest: Average 6 L/min
  靜止時正常值：平均 6 公升/分鐘
• Normal with exercise: Up to 200 L/min
  運動時正常值：高達 200 公升/分鐘
• Only rough estimate of respiratory efficiency 僅粗略估計呼吸效率
• MV = TV × Respiratory Rate 每分鐘通氣量 = 潮氣容積 × 呼吸頻率
• Example: TV = 500 ml; Respiratory Rate = 14 breaths/min
 例子：潮氣容積 = 500 毫升; 呼吸頻率 = 14 次呼吸/分鐘
                      MV = 500 × 14 = 7000 ml/min
                   每分鐘通氣量 = 500 × 14 = 7000 毫升/分鐘

• Partial pressures and gas concentrations分壓和氣體濃度
• (1) The pressure exerted by each gas (Partial Pressure) in a mixture of gases is directly
    proportional to the percentage of the gas in the mixture.
    氣體混合物中每種氣體施加的壓力(分壓)與混合物中氣體的百分比成正比。
•   E.g. 21% O2 & 760 mmHg             PO2 = 160 mmHg
    例如 21% 氧氣 和 760 mmHg 氧氣分壓 = 160 mmHg
•   (2) When a mixture of gases dissolves in liquid, the concentration of each gas in the liquid will
    be directly proportional to its partial pressure.
    當氣體混合物溶解在液體中時，液體中每種氣體的濃度將與其分壓成正比。
• i.e. higher partial pressure ⇒ higher concentration 即更高的分壓⇒更高的濃度
• partial pressure gradient ⇒ concentration gradient 分壓梯度⇒濃度梯度
• When gas diffuse across the respiratory membrane 當氣體擴散穿過呼吸膜
• Region of higher partial pressure → Region of lower partial pressure
  分壓較高的區域 → 分壓較低的區域
• Down the partial pressure gradient!!! 沿著分壓梯度向下!!

• Molecular O2 is carried in blood in two ways:

   每個 Hb 分子由四條多肽鏈組成，每條多肽鏈有 1 個含鐵血紅素基團

   脫氧血紅蛋白 (HHb)：已釋放出氧分子的血紅蛋白
