2Background: What is physiology?
背景：什麼是生理學？
What is “physiology”? 什麼是「生理學」？
• Physiology is scientific study of: 生理學是關於以下方面的科學研究：
• normal conditions and activities of a living organism
生物的正常狀態和活動
• and functions and mechanisms of body parts for maintaining survival.
及維持生存所需的身體不同部位的功能和機制。
• Content of “human physiology”:「人體生理學」內容：
• (1) Normal conditions of human body 人體的正常狀態
• (2) Functions of human cells, tissues, and organs for achieving normal
conditions 達到人體正常狀態所需的細胞、組織和器官的功能

4Organization of Human Body
人體的組織結構
• The organizationof the human body can be broken down into several levels, from the
simplest to the most complex, namely:
人體的組織結構可以分解成多個層次，從最簡單到最複雜，分別是：
• Chemical level: different chemicals (E.g. proteins)
化學層面：不同的化學物質(例如蛋白質)
• Cellular level: single cell
細胞層面：單一細胞
• Tissue level: groups of similar cells
組織層面：由相似的細胞形成的細胞群體
• Organ level: discrete structure, which contains two or more types of tissues, for specific functions
器官層面：由兩個或以上的組織組成的獨立結構，用來執行特定的功能
• Organ system level: several organs that work closely together
系統層面：緊密協作的多個器官
• Organism level: all organ systems combined to make the whole organism
生物體層面：所有器官系統共同構成整個生物體

• Water水
• Most abundantinorganiccompound (60%–80% of volume of living cells)
最豐富的無機化合物(佔活細胞的體積60%–80%)
• Important functions: 重要功能：
• (1) Useful solvent 用作溶劑
• Dissolves many substances for chemical reactions in body
溶解許多用於體內化學反應的物質
• Body’s major transport medium身體的主要運輸介質
• (2) Stabilize body temperature 穩定體溫
• Example: Sweating 例如：出汗
• (3) Cushioning to protect organs 保護器官的緩衝
• Example: cerebrospinal fluid cushions nervous system organs
例如：腦脊髓液為神經系統器官提供緩衝
• (4) Lubricant to enhance movement 用作潤滑劑協助活動
• Example: synovial fluid in joints 例如：關節滑液
