• Two major types of active membrane transport methods
兩種主要類型的主動膜運輸方法
• Active transport 主動運輸
• Vesicular transport 囊泡運輸/膜泡運輸
• Both require cellular energy to move molecules across plasma
membrane for any of these reasons:
兩者都需要細胞能量來移動分子穿過細胞膜，原因如下：
• Molecule is too large for channel proteins 分子對於通道蛋白來說太大
• Molecule is not lipid soluble 分子不溶於脂質
• Molecule is not able to move down concentration gradient
分子無法沿濃度梯度向下移動

20Membrane Transport and Membrane Potential
跨膜運輸與膜電位
• Selective permeability of cell membrane leads to different concentrations of
charged particles in cytoplasm and in extracellular fluid. This results in
membrane potential, the difference of voltage across the cell membrane.
細胞膜的選擇性通透性導致細胞質和細胞外液中帶電粒子濃度不同，這導致
膜電位，即細胞膜內外的電壓差。
• In resting state, resting membrane potentials of all human cells range from -
50mV to -100mV, depending on cell type.
在靜息狀態下，所有人體細胞的靜息膜電位範圍為 -50mV 至 -100mV，具體
取決於細胞類型。
• negative sign (-) indicates inside of cell is more negative than outside of cell
負號(-)表示細胞內部比細胞外部電壓更負
21Membrane Transport and Membrane Potential
跨膜運輸與膜電位
• Generation and Maintaining of Resting Membrane Potential
產生和維持靜息膜電位
• (1) Proteins with negative charge are too large and polar. They cannot pass across
cell membrane and will be trapped inside cell.
帶負電荷的蛋白質分子太大且極性太強，不能穿過細胞膜，會被困在細胞內。
• (2) Cell membrane is somehow more permeable to K+. K+ are transported out of cells
by facilitateddiffusion.
細胞膜某程度上對鉀離子的滲透性更強，鉀離子通過促進擴散從細胞中轉運出來。
• (3) Action of Na+/K+ pump brings more ions with positive charge out of cell.
鈉鉀泵的活動將更多帶正電荷的離子帶出細胞。
• Change of membrane potential is very important for
the function of muscular system and nervous system.
膜電位的變化對肌肉系統和神經系統的功能非常重要。
