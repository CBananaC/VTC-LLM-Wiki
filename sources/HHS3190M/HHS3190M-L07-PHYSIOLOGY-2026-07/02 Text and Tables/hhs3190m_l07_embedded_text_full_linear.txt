• Chemical structureof a hormone determines its solubility in water. This is related to:
激素的化學結構決定了其在水中的溶解度，這與以下有關：
• How is it transportedin blood? 它是如何在血液中運輸的？
• How does it affectits targetcells? 它如何影響其靶細胞？
• Two classes:兩個類別：
• (1) Protein or amino acid based 基於蛋白質或氨基酸
Most hormones are proteins or amino acid based. (E.g. LH, Epi, ADH)
大多數激素是基於蛋白質或氨基酸的。(例如LH、Epi、ADH)
These are usually water soluble and cannot cross cell membrane.
它們通常是水溶性的，不能穿過細胞膜。
• (2) Steroids 類固醇
Some hormones are made from cholesterol. (E.g. aldosterone, estrogen, testosterone)
有些激素是由膽固醇製成的。(例如醛固酮、雌激素、睪固酮)
These are all lipid soluble and can cross cell membrane.
這些都是脂溶性的，可以穿過細胞膜。

• Hormones are carried by blood to all tissues.
However, not all cells are affected by 1 hormone.
血液運輸激素到全身組織，但並非所有細胞都受到
1種激素的影響。
• The cells, that are affected, are “target cells” of a
certain hormone.
受影響的細胞被稱為某種激素的“靶細胞”。
• These cells have specific receptor proteins in cytoplasm
or in cell membrane.
這些細胞在細胞質或細胞膜中具有特定的受體蛋白。
• Hormone molecules bind to receptor proteins and
trigger a series of changes of these cells.
激素分子與受體蛋白結合並引發這些細胞的一系列變化。
