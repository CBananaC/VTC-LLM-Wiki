Hormone Chemical Structure 激素化學結構
• Chemical structure of a hormone determines its solubility in water. This is related to:
  激素的化學結構決定了其在水中的溶解度，這與以下有關：
   • How is it transported in blood? 它是如何在血液中運輸的？
   • How does it affect its target cells? 它如何影響其靶細胞？
• Two classes:兩個類別：
    • (1) Protein or amino acid based 基於蛋白質或氨基酸
    Most hormones are proteins or amino acid based. (E.g. LH, Epi, ADH)
    大多數激素是基於蛋白質或氨基酸的。(例如LH、Epi、ADH)
    These are usually water soluble and cannot cross cell membrane.
    它們通常是水溶性的，不能穿過細胞膜。
    • (2) Steroids 類固醇
    Some hormones are made from cholesterol. (E.g. aldosterone, estrogen, testosterone)
    有些激素是由膽固醇製成的。(例如醛固酮、雌激素、睪固酮)
    These are all lipid soluble and can cross cell membrane.
    這些都是脂溶性的，可以穿過細胞膜。

Mechanisms of Hormone Action 激素作用機制
• Mechanisms of water soluble hormones:
  水溶性激素的機制：
   • (1) Bind to “plasma membrane receptors” on cell membrane
     與細胞膜上的「細胞表面受體」結合
   • (2) Trigger increase of different “second messengers” inside the cell
     觸發細胞內部不同的「第二信使」的增加
       • Examples of common second messengers include cAMP, calcium ions, cGMP, etc.
         常見的第二信使的例子包括環腺苷酸、鈣離子、環磷酸鳥苷等。
   • (3) Second messengers activate or inactivate enzymes inside cytoplasm.
     第二信使激活或失活細胞質內的酶。
   • (4) Cellular activities are changed!
     細胞活動改變！

Mechanisms of Hormone Action 激素作用機制
• Mechanisms of lipid soluble hormones:
  脂溶性激素的機制：
   • (1) Bind to “intracellular receptors” in cytoplasm or in nucleus
     與細胞質或細胞核中的「細胞內受體」結合
   • (2) Hormone-receptor complex binds to a specific DNA region
      激素受體複合物與特定去氧核糖核酸(DNA)區域結合
   • (3) A specific mRNA is produced from certain region of DNA.
     特定的信使核糖核酸(mRNA )由核糖核酸(DNA)的某個區域產生。
   • (4) The mRNA leaves nucleus and attaches to ribosomes on rough ER.
     信使核糖核酸(mRNA )離開細胞核並附著在粗面內質網上的核糖體上。
   • (5) New proteins are produced to change cellular activities.
     產生新的蛋白質來改變細胞活動。

Regulation of Hormone Secretion 激素分泌的調節
• The synthesis and release of most hormones are regulated by some type of negative
  feedback mechanism. 大多數激素的合成和釋放受到某種負反饋機制的調節。
• 3 types of stimuli trigger endocrine glands to release more hormones.
  3 類刺激會觸發內分泌腺釋放更多激素。
    • (1) Humoral stimuli 體液刺激:
      concentrations of certain molecules (E.g. glucose) or ions (E.g. Calcium ions) in blood
      血液中某些分子(例如葡萄糖)或離子(例如鈣離子)的濃度
        • “Low Na+ or high K+ blood levels 低血鈉或高血鉀”
          ➔ Adrenal gland secretes more aldosterone腎上腺分泌更多的醛固酮
          ➔ Increase Na+ and decrease K+ 增鈉及減鉀
    • (2) Neural stimuli 神經刺激: ANS controls endocrine glands 自主神經系統控制內分泌腺
        • “Sympathetic nervous system交感神經系統”
          ➔ Adrenal gland secretes more epinephrine (Epi) 腎上腺分泌較多的腎上腺素
          ➔ Enhance “Fight-or-flight” responses 增強「戰鬥或逃跑」反應
    • (3) Hormonal stimuli 激素刺激:
      concentrations of other hormones in blood 血液中其他激素的濃度                                      17
