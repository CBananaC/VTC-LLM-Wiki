  • Head, eyes, and toes directed anteriorly/ forward
     頭部、眼睛和腳趾向前

   • Lower limb close together with feet parallel & toes directed
     anteriorly
     下肢併攏，雙腳掌平行，腳趾向前

      Terminology Related to Positions
            與位置相關的術語
Ipsilateral 同側
  • refers to the same side of the body 指身體的同一側

Bilateral 雙側
  • paired structures in both right & left sides 左右兩側的成對結構

Contralateral 對側
  • refers to opposite sides of the body指身體的相對兩側

Supine 仰臥
  • lying face upward 面朝上躺著

Prone 俯臥
  • lying face downward 面朝下躺著
  Terminology Related to Movements
          與動作相關的術語
Flexion 屈曲- F, Flex
  • Joint bending movement takes place in a
    sagittal plane
    發生在矢狀平面上的關節彎曲活動
  • decreasing in joint angle 關節角度減小
Extension 伸展- Ext
  • movement along sagittal plane that straighten the
    joint with increasing in joint angle.
    沿矢狀平面伸直關節的活動，使關節角度增加
Lateral flexion 側屈 - LF
  • Movement of the trunk in the coronal plane
    軀幹在冠狀平面上的活動
           Terminology Related to Movements
                   與動作相關的術語
 Abduction 外展 -Abd
       •    The limb moving away from midline of the body, along the
            coronal plane 肢體沿冠狀平面從身體中線向外移動

Adduction 內收- Add
   •       The limb moving towards body in the coronal plane
           肢體沿冠狀平面向身體移動

Rotation 旋轉 - Rot

• Turning of a bone along its longitudinal axis 骨骼沿其縱軸旋轉
    Terminology Related to Movements
            與動作相關的術語
Medial/ Internal rotation 內旋- MR/ IR
    •   Movement that results in the anterior surface of the body part
        facing medially
        導致身體部位前表面朝向中間的動作

Lateral/ External Rotation 外旋 – LR/ ER
    •   Movement that results in the anterior surface of the body part
        facing laterally
        導致身體部位前表面朝向側面的動作
    Circumduction 環轉
•   Joint movement that combines Flexion, Abduction, Extension
    and Adduction sequentially
    依次結合屈曲、外展、伸展和內收的關節運動

Dorsiflexion 背屈 – DF
Movement at sagittal plane, lifting foot that dorsum approaches the shin.
矢狀平面的動作，抬起腳背接近脛骨

Plantarflexion 蹠屈 – PF
movement at sagittal plane, depressing foot.
矢狀平面的動作，下壓腳部。

Inversion 內翻- Inv
movement of foot so the sole turns medially
腳部的動作，使腳底轉向中間

Eversion 內翻- Ever
movement of foot so the sole faces laterally
腳部的運動，使腳底朝向側面

  Terminologies related to Bone Markings
         與骨骼標記相關的術語
Condyle髁
  •   rounded articular projection (e.g. femoral condyle), articulates with
      corresponding fossa
      關節位置的圓形突起（例如 股骨髁），與相應的關節窩相對
Epicondyle上髁
  •   eminence superior to a condyle 髁上方之隆起
Crest 嵴
  •   narrow ridge of bone, usually prominent (e.g. iliac crest)
      狹窄的骨脊，通常突出（例如 髂嵴）
Malleolus踝
  •   rounded prominence (e.g. lateral malleolus)
      圓形突起（例如 外踝）
       Terminologies related to Bone Markings
              與骨骼標記相關的術語
Spine 棘
   •    Thorn-like process(e.g. scapula spine)/sharp, slender often pointed
        projection (e.g. spinous process)
        棘狀突（如肩胛岡）/銳利、細長而通常尖的突起（如棘突）
Tubercle 結節
   •    Small rounded process/ projection 小而圓的突起
        e.g. greater tubercle of the humerus 例如 肱骨大結節
Tuberosity 粗隆
   •    Large rounded elevation e.g. tibial tuberosity
        大的圓形隆起，例如脛骨粗隆
Trochanter 轉子
   •    Large blunt elevation, irregular shape process
        大而鈍的隆起，不規則的形狀的突起
        e.g. greater trochanter of femur 例如：股骨大轉子
