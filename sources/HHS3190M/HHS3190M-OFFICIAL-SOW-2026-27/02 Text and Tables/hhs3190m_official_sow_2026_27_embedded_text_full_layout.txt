Module Intended Learning Outcomes (MILO) 單元預期學習成果:

On completion of this module, learners are expected to be able to:

   1. Describe the mechanism of human body in maintaining a normal homeostasis or accomplishing a
       particular task essential for life with reference to the functions of various body systems regarding the
       rehabilitation industry.
   2. Explain the consequences of different various physiological disorders with appropriate mechanisms in
       account of rehabilitation aspects.
   3. Identify, classify and describe the musculoskeletal structures of the human body in standard
       terminologies in rehabilitation perspective.
   4. Illustrate the interaction between responsible musculoskeletal structures of human body involving in
       the complex functional movements in rehabilitation perspective.
   5. Apply the essential concepts of human physiology and functional anatomy regarding the rehabilitation
       perspective.

完成本單元後, 學生預期能夠學習 :

   1. 描述人體在維持正常穩態或完成生命所需特定任務的機制，並參考各個身體系統在復康行業
       中的功能 。
   2. 從復康的角度，以合適之機制解釋不同生理疾病的後果。
   3. 從復康的角度，及使用標準術語識別、分類並描述人體的肌肉骨骼結構。
   4. 從復康的角度，說明人體中負責複雜功能運動的肌肉骨骼結構之間的相互作用。
   5. 從復康的角度，應用有關人體生理學和功能解剖學的基本概念。

In order to pass this module, a student must:

(a) attain at least 70% attendance at the end of this module in semester 2; and
(b) pass End-of-Module Assessment (EA) by attaining at least 40% of overall EA mark in semester 2; and
(c) attain at least 40% of overall module mark.

為了通過本單元，學生必須：

(a) 在第二學期本單元結束時，出席率至少達到 70%; 及
(b) 通過單元總評核（EA）在第二學期達到至少 40% 的 EA 總分; 及
(c) 達到至少 40% 的單元總分。

Note 注意:

Application of Low Attendance Approval: Teacher will not record attendance for student. Student is
responsible for his/her own attendance record. For any student taking leave with valid reason and failing to
attain minimum attendance requirement, he/she should apply for low attendance approval from campus
principals by submitting true copy of supporting document (E.g. Sick Leave Certificate*).
申請低出席率許可：教師不會代為記錄學生的出席情況，學生必須對自己的出席記錄負責，任何學
生因正當理由請假而未能達到最低出席率要求，應提交證明文件（例如病假證明書†）的正本向校長
申請低出席率許可。

Application of Re-Assessment: For any student absent in CA Test or EA Assessments with valid reason,
he/she should apply for re-assessment by submitting true copy of supporting document (E.g. Sick Leave
Certificate) to teacher within 3 working days. If the application is approved, the re-assessment of CA will be
conducted as soon as possible and EA re-assessment will be conducted during the next available round of
the applied assessment.
申請補考：任何學生因正當理由缺席 CA 測驗或單元總評核，應在 3 個工作日內向老師提交證明文
件（例如病假證明書）的正本申請補考，如申請獲批，CA 補考將儘快安排，單元總評核之補考將在
下一輪的單元總評核中進行。
